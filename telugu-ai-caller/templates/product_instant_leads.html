For developers

An API in, a webhook out.

Send leads from any system with one POST. Get every call back as a signed webhook: transcript, recording, outcome and the details captured.

Lead intake API
POST a lead. We call it.
202 Accepted
# A new lead → a call within seconds
curl -X POST https://api.outpero.com/lead-intake/<your-slug> \
  -H "X-Outpero-Lead-Secret: <secret>" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "phone": "+919812345437", "lead_name": "Priya", "interest": "2BHK, Baner" }'
  • · Fields matching your pre-call variables are used on the call ("Hi Priya…").
  • · Duplicate leads within minutes are ignored, so retries never double-dial.
  • · Leads outside your calling hours are called when your hours open.
