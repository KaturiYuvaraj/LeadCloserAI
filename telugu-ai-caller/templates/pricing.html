Questions
What exactly am I paying for?+

Connected talk time, by the minute, at your voice tier's rate. There is no monthly subscription and no setup fee.

Do credits expire?+

No. One credit is one rupee, and unused credits stay in your wallet.

What is the ₹649 a month?+

Your dedicated business number, with one channel (one call at a time). It is deducted from your credits each month, and covers compliance and carrier fees.

What is a channel?+

A channel is one call at a time on your number. With 1 channel your AI telecaller makes one call at a time; with 5 it makes five at once. Each extra channel is ₹649/month, and you can add or remove them any month.

Do you offer volume pricing?+

Yes. For high call volumes, custom rates go as low as ₹3.5/min. Talk to us and we'll quote for your volume.

Can I try it before paying?+

Yes. You get 50 free credits on signup, no card required.

Can I change the voice tier later?+

Any time, per employee. The new rate applies from the next call.

Are prices inclusive of GST?+

No. Prices shown exclude 18% GST.
